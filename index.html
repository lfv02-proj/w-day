V · Make a weekend of it

A little more
of Mikulov.

For everyone travelling from another city or country, Mikulov is worth an unhurried weekend. These official guides will help you plan walks, tastings, and a day beyond town.

01In town

Historic Mikulov

Begin at the main square, then wander through the protected old town past Baroque facades, the Dietrichstein Tomb, the Jewish Quarter, and viewpoints below the chateau.

Official town guide ↗
02Sunset walk

Svatý Kopeček

Holy Hill is Mikulov's signature climb. Allow comfortable shoes and time for the chapels, limestone landscape, and panoramic views across vineyards and the Pálava Hills.

Plan the walk ↗
03Local flavour

Moravian wine

Limestone slopes and a warm climate shape the Mikulov wine region. Look for local Riesling, Welschriesling and Pálava, and reserve cellar tastings ahead for a larger group.

Wine guide ↗
04Half or full day

Lednice & Valtice

Explore a UNESCO-listed cultural landscape of chateaux, gardens, ponds, romantic follies, forest paths, and vineyards. It is an easy extension to a South Moravian weekend.

Explore the landscape ↗
